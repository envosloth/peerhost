- Preserve the original issue prose, updating checkboxes only for verified completion with an accompanying evidence report.

## Server settings, metrics, group lifecycle, and in-guide friends — delivered locally (alpha-3l9eXo)

- **Server settings:** added **Hardcore** (plus spawn-protection and allow-flight) beside every existing control; each displayed property saves, reads back on disk, and survives reopening a disposable profile. Edited-but-empty numeric fields are refused instead of silently saved as 0; unknown keys stay untouched. (Minecraft itself only enforces Hardcore with difficulty=hard.)
- **Performance tab:** CPU/memory values display at most 2 decimal places; unavailable samples remain “Unavailable”, never rounded fabrications.
- **Multi-host disband:** a **Disband group** action with native confirmation performs real relay-wide revocation — members and outstanding invitations are invalidated durably at the group relay; worlds, backups, accounts, friendships, Playit routes, and the app-wide always-on opt-in are retained. It refuses unfinished custody/handoff or when another local server shares the group. After disband, **Create a group** makes a **fresh distinct group identity** (new OS-vault identity + storage namespace); the revoked identity stays tombstoned and old invitations stay dead.
- **Always-on PC is optional:** creating a group starts group control only; the always-on player gateway/custodian is an explicit opt-in in Multi-host, never auto-enabled. Existing opt-ins are preserved and not rotated by fresh-group creation.
- **Setup guide friends:** signed-in users can send and accept friend requests inline in the guide’s Friends step — it no longer bounces to the Friends tab mid-guide.
- **Review findings fixed (independent reviewer, fail-closed):** (1) disband now serializes with and drains already-admitted public-address mutations and refuses newly queued ones after revocation (RED regression added, then GREEN); (2) the permanent-tombstone lifecycle gap (no replacement group possible) fixed by group-generation rotation with tombstones intact.

Verified by reading the live Playit account through the app's approved agent: tunnels `pgsql-nic` (→25565) and `pgsql-bonded` (→25566, the app-wide one), with account notice “Please verify your email address”. Independent final re-review `deleg_de827bf7` passed with no security concerns or logic errors.
