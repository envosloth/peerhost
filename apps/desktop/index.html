              Friends optional
              Friends in a group can take turns hosting the same world: whoever is online starts it, and the world moves safely between your PCs. A group lives on an always-on PC (next step).
              • Got an invitation code? Paste it below to join.
              • Starting a group? Set up the always-on PC first, then invite friends from here or the Friends tab.
              • Just playing on your own Wi-Fi? Skip this — friends can join your server directly.
              
        
              Always-on PC optional
              A spare computer that stays on — an old laptop, mini PC or home server — can store your world between hosts and give everyone one address to join, even when you’re offline.
              
          
              • One address for everyoneFriends join the same address, no matter who’s hosting.
              • The world waits safelyIt stays on that PC between play sessions.
              • OptionalJust want to play now? Skip this and come back any time.
              
          
              Step-by-step: set up the always-on PC (about 10 minutes, uses a terminal)
              1. On the always-on PC: install Node.js 22 or newer and Seed Hosting’s relay tool. Find that PC’s address on your network (or Tailscale) and use it in place of RELAY_ADDRESS below. Don’t use 0.0.0.0 as the address you share.
              2. Start it in a terminal:
                peerhost-relay init --root ./peerhost-relay
peerhost-relay serve --root ./peerhost-relay --host 0.0.0.0 --port 47625 --advertise RELAY_ADDRESS --game-host 0.0.0.0 --game-port 25565
                Not using the shared player address? Leave out both --game options.
              3. Create the first invitation in a second terminal, while it runs:
                peerhost-relay invite --root ./peerhost-relay --advertise RELAY_ADDRESS --advertise-port 47625
              4. On each gaming PC: go to Friends → I have an invitation code. Check that the fingerprint shown matches the always-on PC before accepting.
              5. First host: stop your server, choose Hand off to always-on PC, then Take over hosting once. Turn on the player address below on every PC that hosts.
              6. Network: allow TCP 47625 (Seed Hosting) and 25565 (players) on the always-on PC if needed. Seed Hosting never changes your router or firewall.
              7. Players join RELAY_ADDRESS:25565 and reconnect when the host changes.
